A LITTLE BACKGROUND

Research mindset.
Engineering instinct.

My work has moved across reinforcement learning, conversational AI, post-training safety, and core machine learning. I’m interested in both what models can do and how we make them useful, reliable, and responsible.

At FAIR, I worked with teams led by Gabriel Synnaeve, Jason Weston, and Yuandong Tian. Before that, I studied machine learning, human–computer interaction, and data science in Paris.
