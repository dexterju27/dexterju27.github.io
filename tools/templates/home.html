A LITTLE BACKGROUND

From dialogue systems
to reasoning and safety.

My research spans deployed dialogue systems such as BlenderBot 3, recurrent architectures such as Staircase Attention, and agentic travel planning with To the Globe. More recently, I worked on RL and reasoning for Llama 4; I now focus on foundation-model safety at Microsoft AI.

At FAIR, I worked with teams led by Gabriel Synnaeve, Jason Weston, and Yuandong Tian. Before that, I studied machine learning, human–computer interaction, and data science in Paris.
